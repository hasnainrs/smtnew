Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Sports Medicine Training (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses and discloses information on sportsmedicinetraining.com and the services, courses and webinars we offer (together, the “Services”).

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy covers our processing of Personal Data collected when you access or use our Services. “Personal Data” means any information that can be used to identify an individual, and “processing” includes collection, use, storage and disclosure.

This policy also covers Personal Data shared with trusted service providers and business partners. It does not apply to third-party websites, services or applications that we do not own or control — we encourage you to review their own privacy policies.

If you are a resident of the United Kingdom, European Union, Liechtenstein, Norway or Iceland, you may have additional rights under the UK GDPR / GDPR, outlined below.

What personal data we collect

We collect Personal Data when you provide it directly, when third parties provide it to us, or automatically through your use of the Services.

Information you provide directly:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Country of residence
  • Billing information (processed securely by third-party payment providers)

Payment information. Payments for courses and webinars are processed by third-party payment processors. Payment details are stored only by the processor, not by us — please review their privacy policy too.

Information from third-party sources: social media or third-party login services (if used); marketing and analytics partners; advertising platforms.

Automatically collected: IP address; browser and device information; page views and browsing activity; usage and click-tracking data; transaction metadata; cookies and similar technologies; access logs.

Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our Services, analyse user behaviour, and measure marketing effectiveness. Cookies may be session or persistent; you can control cookie preferences through your browser settings, though disabling cookies may affect functionality. Categories in use may include essential, functionality, performance/analytics, and advertising/retargeting cookies. We do not control third-party cookies placed by external services.

For more on managing cookies, visit the UK ICO website or allaboutcookies.org.

Interest-based advertising

We may work with advertising partners to deliver relevant advertising based on your browsing behaviour, using cookies, pixels or web beacons. We do not share Personal Data with advertisers without consent. You can opt out of interest-based advertising via aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu.

How we use your personal data

We process Personal Data to communicate about courses, webinars and services; manage registrations and waitlists; deliver digital educational content; process payments; improve website performance; prevent fraud and misuse; provide customer support; comply with legal obligations; and enforce this Privacy Policy.

Lawful bases: contractual necessity, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligations.

How we share your data

We may share Personal Data with payment processors, hosting and infrastructure providers, email and marketing platforms, analytics providers, and professional advisers. We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our rights, users or Services. In the event of a business sale, merger or restructuring, Personal Data may transfer as part of that transaction.

Data retention

We retain Personal Data for as long as necessary to provide the Services, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Data may later be anonymised or aggregated.

Security measures

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from individuals under the age of 16. If we become aware that such data has been collected, it will be deleted promptly.

Your rights (UK GDPR / GDPR)

You have the right to access your Personal Data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, withdraw consent, request data portability, object to processing, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

International data transfers

Your Personal Data may be processed outside the UK or EU where appropriate safeguards are in place.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your Personal Data, please get in touch via our contact form, or write to us at info@radiologyseminars.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Inside the programme

What the 10 modules cover

Taught joint by joint, from imaging fundamentals through to the medical work-up.

What shows up on each imaging sequence; osteoarthritis, painful tendons, tears and inflammatory conditions; X-ray quiz. Then evidence updates on frozen shoulder, a live shoulder MRI tutorial, rotator cuff tears and tendinopathy, calcific tendinopathy, and the key views to show your patient.

Tennis and golfer's elbow, cubital tunnel syndrome and OA; live elbow MRI tutorial; ultrasound work-up and treatment options including shockwave, steroid and PRP. Then De Quervain's, scaphoid, TFCC, ECU tendinopathy, trigger finger, Dupuytren's and the hand arthritis work-up.

Live spine MRI tutorial; sports trauma, fractures, OA, ankylosing spondylitis, sciatica and disc disease; back pain in the young athlete; radicular pain and radiculopathy.

Knee OA; live knee MRI tutorial; fractures, dislocations, meniscus injuries, ACL/PCL, MCL/LCL and osteochondral defects; evidence updates on ACL and meniscus injury. Then live ankle MRI tutorial, Achilles, osteochondral injuries, sinus tarsi and tarsal tunnel syndrome, and lateral ankle injuries.

Live foot & ankle MRI tutorial; gout, stress fractures, Lisfranc and midfoot injuries, sesamoiditis and metatarsalgia; bone stress injuries and exercise-induced leg pain; plantar heel pain. Then live hip MRI tutorial, FAI, OA, greater trochanteric pain syndrome, labral injuries and deep gluteal pain syndrome.

Exertional rhabdomyolysis, and the gut–joint axis in the exercising patient.

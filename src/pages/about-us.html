About us

The gap we exist to close

Sports medicine clinicians keep asking the same question: how do I bring clinical assessment, MRI interpretation and ultrasound into one coherent skillset? Traditional CPD rarely joins those dots. Our programmes are built to do exactly that.

Who we are

One mission, three centres of expertise

Sports Medicine Training grew out of a collaboration between Radiology Seminars, the MSK Playbook educational series, and the Advanced MSK Ultrasound Centre.

Our name changed to better reflect that focused mission, but the commitment hasn't: exceptional education for sports medicine clinicians, delivered by the same team — now with broader expertise and a bigger ambition for how many clinicians we can reach.
